03 / Fast checks

Clue words are safer than a name alone

The collected solution material warns that a Cipher name and a clue word can point to different lookups. Read the full item text before selecting Decipher.

  • Inspect the complete description
  • Note both thematic words
  • Match the NPC family
  • Choose Decipher at camp
  • Recheck after updates

02 / Free-to-play question

Is Mistfall Hunter free?

The two primary collected price references describe Mistfall Hunter as a paid game rather than a free-to-play release. A separate video transcript discusses additional access options, but the local collection does not provide a stable official statement to use as a universal price rule.

What the collected material supports

  • Standard and Deluxe are paid editions.
  • The base-game price in the store snapshot is $24.99.
  • Deluxe is listed at $39.99.
  • Regional pricing and promotions need a fresh store check.

Which edition should you compare?

Use Standard when you are comparing the entry cost of the base game. Use Deluxe only when the listed cosmetic items and 2,500 Fate Coins justify the difference for you. The collected editorial source says Standard buyers can upgrade later, but that upgrade path should still be checked against the current store listing.

Other store items in the collected listing

The PlayStation material also shows Fate Coin add-ons at several prices. Those are optional virtual currency listings and are separate from the base-game edition comparison.

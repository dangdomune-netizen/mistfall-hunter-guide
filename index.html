02 / Solo comparison

Collected Solo tier snapshot

Solo play shifts the weighting toward mobility and self-sufficiency because there is no teammate to cover a weak matchup.

TierClassCollected reason
SShadowstrixStealth and dash tools create burst and escape options.
SBlackarrowCan kite melee threats and remain useful in both PvP and PvE encounters.
AWithered KnightVersatile in PvE, with more trouble against ranged classes.
AMercenaryReliable and self-contained, but exposed when opponents kite.
ASorcererEffective against melee fighters but harder to use alone in PvE.
BSeerTeam healing has less value alone and the source describes lower solo damage.

Why the two lists diverge

The collected comparison identifies a simple split: safe sustained damage is more valuable when teammates create space, while mobility and self-sufficiency matter more when a player has to handle every encounter alone.

That is why Sorcerer and Blackarrow rise in the Trio table, while Shadowstrix rises in the Solo table. A class lower in one mode can still fit a player better when its weapon loop and decision-making match the queue.

02 / How to read it

Steam is a platform slice

The collected material says no public number in the source set covers Xbox or PlayStation concurrent sessions. Steam figures are therefore useful activity signals, but they are not a complete cross-platform census.

ConcurrentPlayers in the game at a moment or recorded interval.
PeakThe highest recorded concurrent value in the stated window or history.
AverageA calculated period summary, not the number online right now.
Total playersA launch or lifetime milestone is not interchangeable with concurrent players.

Why the trackers disagree

A reading taken at 00:08 UTC, a 24-hour high and a month-to-date average answer three different questions. Third-party trackers also use different collection intervals and estimation methods.

The local material includes a separate launch milestone discussion, but those total-player statements are not used as a current concurrent count. Keep platform, time window and measurement label attached to every number.

When this page needs a fresh check

Refresh the figures when the source timestamp is older than the decision you are trying to make, after a major update or when comparing matchmaking activity across platforms.

03 / Player count FAQ

The questions behind the number

How many people are playing Mistfall Hunter?

The collected material does not contain a live all-platform count. It records dated Steam snapshots, including 14,103 at one Raijin reading on August 21, 2026 at 00:08 UTC and a PlayerAuctions 24-hour range of 10,341 to 19,555 on August 20.

Does the player count include Xbox and PlayStation?

No public concurrent figure in the collected sources covers those platforms. The displayed snapshots are Steam measurements.

What was the all-time peak?

Raijin's collected August 21 snapshot lists 44,201 as an all-time Steam peak. Keep that separate from daily highs and monthly averages.

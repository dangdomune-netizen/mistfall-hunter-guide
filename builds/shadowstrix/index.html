Mistfall Hunter Shadowstrix Build

Shadowstrix is built around stealth, burst, mobility and precise timing. The collected build material separates a Dagger path for PvE from a Dual Blades path for PvP pressure.

Build referencePvE and PvP branches
Execution warning

The source describes low survivability, tight combo timing and difficult melee matchups. Treat stealth as a setup and escape tool, not permission to stay in every fight.

01 / Class identity

Disappear, reposition, commit

Poison Crow Mark slows movement and dodge distance, Crow Storm creates a damage zone, and Sneak lets the class approach or reset. The collected material frames the class as especially threatening to ranged and caster opponents, while melee pressure is a major risk.

StrengthsBurst damage, fast attacks, stealth and mobility.
Trade-offLow health, complex combos and punishment for mistakes.
Shared skillsPoison Crow Mark and Crow Storm.
Weapon pathsDagger and Dual Blades.

PvE: Dagger route

The collected guide recommends Dagger for more consistent PvE damage, reliable stealth and control over boss or minion engagements.

Core PvE skills

  • Shadow Wheel Slash for the three-stage damage chain and Energy-restoring branches.
  • Sneak to approach, skip a dangerous pack or reset a losing fight.
  • Poison Crow Mark to reduce movement and dodge distance before the follow-up.
  • Flash Stride for damage and repositioning.

Key PvE talents

  • Ankle Cut slows enemies hit by Shadow Wheel Slash.
  • Shadow Slash reduces Sneak cooldown through the branching attack.
  • Bloodthirst resets Sneak after a kill and restores Energy.
  • Sneak Prep restores Energy when Poison Crow Mark connects.

Practice the timing window between Shadow Wheel Slash stages; the source warns that missing the continuation resets the chain.

PvP: Dual Blades route

The Dual Blades path adds faster pressure, aggressive mobility and Phantom Shift. The collected setup uses mobility to choose the burst window, then exits before the class is trapped in a prolonged melee exchange.

Core PvP skills

  • Pursuit Strike to close distance with a rapid multi-hit attack.
  • Flurry Strike to retreat and dash back in, with a charge option for more distance and damage.
  • Bloody Blade Dance for continuous hits and projectile deflection.
  • Fang Rush after Dodge for another dash-and-damage window.
  • Crow Storm to deny an escape route or force movement.
  • Phantom Shift to teleport and gain movement speed after the move.

Key PvP talents and affixes

The collected PvP notes name Lightweight, Bloodthirsty Frenzy, Enhanced Crow Storm and Combat Euphoria. Fervor, Seeker and Stoic are the listed affix directions, with Stoic especially relevant to the class's low survivability.

Mistfall Hunter Sorcerer Build

The collected Sorcerer guide favors a Stardust-focused setup: broad area damage and control supported by defensive general spells, with Chanting and positioning treated as the central constraints.

Build referenceStardust focus
Skill ceiling

The source describes Sorcerer as powerful but complicated. The setup below is a collected recommendation, not a guarantee of best results for every player or mode.

01 / Magic focus

Why the collected guide chooses Stardust

The Stardust tree is described as offering wider area damage and crowd control. Elemental Magic remains a viable alternative in the source, with precise options such as Thunder Pierce, Thunderstorm Sigil and Deep Freeze.

General

Windcraft

An opening cyclone that knocks enemies back without a Chant.

Defense

Cryptic Ward

A defensive spell whose Perfect Block can knock back and stun.

Escape

Shadow Veil

A short Chant enters Stealth to evade a bad position or avoid a direct engage.

Stardust

Area control

Meteor Charm, Gravitational Vortex and Meteor Impact form the collected damage core.

Collected six-spell setup

The source recommends three General spells and three Stardust spells:

  • Windcraft
  • Cryptic Ward
  • Shadow Veil
  • Meteor Charm
  • Gravitational Vortex
  • Meteor Impact

Meteor Charm is the repeatable primary attack in the collected setup. Gravitational Vortex pulls targets together and can stun groups; Meteor Impact knocks targets up and creates space when they close in.

Talents for the Chant loop

Chant Guard, Stardust Arcana and Stardust Reversal support the Stardust casting window. Memorization shortens the Gravitational Vortex problem, while Swift Strike and No Escape are tied to Meteor Impact and Meteor Charm. Hurricane improves Windcraft knockback, and Survival Instinct helps when Energy is low.

02 / Gear direction

Make time to Chant

The collected budget discussion names Eloquence, HP and Tenacious-related choices. The consistent theme is to reduce the cost of being interrupted or caught while a spell is charging.

EloquenceCollected as the key Chant-speed direction.
HealthGives room to survive a close-range mistake.
TenaciousAppears in the collected gear discussion as a survivability and healing-related option.
PositioningUse Windcraft, Cryptic Ward and Shadow Veil to create casting space.

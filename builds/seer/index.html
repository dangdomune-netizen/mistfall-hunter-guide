Mistfall Hunter Seer Build

Seer has two distinct directions in the collected material: Blasphemer uses the Mace, rune enchantments and Zeal, while Reverent uses the Catalyst for healing, shielding and Construct control.

01 / Choose a path

Blasphemer or Reverent?

Mace

Blasphemer

Rune enchantments, Zeal, magic damage and control. The collected notes frame this as the more aggressive Seer direction.

Catalyst

Reverent

Healing, shielding and Construct-based zone control for players supporting a trio.

Shared need

Manage energy

Basic orb attacks help refill energy; empowered attacks spend charges for stronger effects.

Positioning

Stay at the edge

Chanting and stationary runes reward safe placement, spacing and awareness of melee pressure.

Collected Reverent support setup

The support references prioritize a small group of runes and utility skills. Punishment Rune provides a recurring offensive anchor, Shelter Rune protects allies, and Binding Rune adds hard crowd control. Wind Walker Rune and Intimidation Rune are presented as situational alternatives for movement or displacement.

Skills and runes in the collected notes

  • Punishment Rune
  • Shelter Rune
  • Binding Rune
  • Wind Surge
  • Healing Art
  • Blinding Rune as a PvP utility option

Support talent direction

The material emphasizes Potent Sanctuary, Fortified Guard, Deep Healing, Synergy, Rigidity and Creation for shield strength, healing, construct uptime, crowd control and resource generation.

Affixes to investigate

Eloquence supports faster spell and rune chanting; Tenacious adds health and healing value; Seamless reduces cooldowns. Valor, Stoic and Elusive are also listed in the collected support discussion for power, emergency resistance and dodge economy.

02 / In a fight

Place first, spend second

The collected support material repeatedly returns to timing: place a Construct where it controls space, save Healing Art for a high-stakes hit and use Wind Surge to peel melee threats or interrupt a channel.

Before contactPrepare shields and place a rune where allies can use its zone.
During pressureProtect the focused ally and keep your own spacing from gap closers.
Control windowUse Binding Rune or Blinding Rune when the team can capitalize on the disruption.
Resource checkWeave basic orb attacks into empowered casts so energy does not empty at the wrong time.

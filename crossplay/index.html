FAQ / mistfall hunter crossplay

Mistfall Hunter Crossplay

The collected platform references say PC, PlayStation 5 and Xbox players can play together. The setup uses the in-game social menu and Player ID rather than platform friend lists.

Platform FAQCrossplay / party setup
Quick answer

Yes, the collected sources describe crossplay across PC, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Platform progress and server region are separate questions.

02 / Playing with friends

Use the in-game Player ID

The collected guide says to finish the opening section so the hub and social features become available, then search for a friend's Player ID or nickname in the Friends menu.

  1. 01

    Finish the opening run

    Progress through the tutorial and early introduction until the social menu is available.

  2. 02

    Open Friends

    Use the social menu to search the other player's ID or nickname.

  3. 03

    Invite the player

    After the request is accepted, invite the friend to the party.

03 / Important limits

Crossplay is not the same as cross-progression

Party sizeThe collected sources describe parties of up to three; a duo can disable Fill Party.
Cross-progressionThe MistfallDB material says characters, stash, gold and unlocks stay on the platform where they were created.
Cross-regionThe same source distinguishes platform crossplay from region-locked matchmaking.
Mixed lobbiesThe collected source describes PC and console players sharing PvE and PvP lobbies.

04 / Crossplay FAQ

Common setup questions

Is Mistfall Hunter cross-platform?

Yes. The collected sources use crossplay and cross-platform to describe PC, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S sharing the same pool.

How many players can join a party?

The collected GameRant and MistfallDB references describe up to three players. A duo can leave Fill Party disabled.

Why can I not invite a friend?

Check the in-game Player ID first, then check the server region. The collected source says platform is not the blocker, while region assignment can be.

02 / Class management

Changing class has a few gates

The collected class source says the option becomes available after the tutorial follow-up quest through Dew. It also lists a storage requirement, a Gyldenblod fee, a cooldown and a short reversion period.

01

Finish the early progression

Complete the next quest after the tutorial before looking for the class-change option.

02

Account for the cost and timer
